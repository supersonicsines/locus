01The mark

Col

A summit, a lower knoll and the pass between them, drawn as contours. The pass’s own contour crosses itself, and the point sits on the crossing: Locus is the one place two heights share, where your company and an agent meet.

  • Black lines on a white tile, on every page, the favicon included.
  • Fine contours, with the pass’s own contour as the heavier index line, as on a survey sheet.
  • Drawn once by a pen, 2.2 s, then still. Six drawings, one for each range of sizes.

How it is drawn

As a surveyor inks a sheet. The index contour goes first, in one stroke from the pass round the summit, through the pass and round the knoll. The two contours below the col follow the pen out of the pass. As it crosses the pass the summit’s contours follow, each from its tip, then the knoll’s. The point is set on the crossing last. A line once drawn never moves again, and nothing loops. With reduced motion the mark simply appears.

Every size is its own drawing

Shown at true size on this screen. Each size gets the drawing its device pixels can hold: the full sheet from 256, fewer contours down to 48, and below that a solid land with the crossing cut through it, where a fine line would blur. The files in logo/mark are named by the size each is drawn for.

On a light page: the light files
On a dark page: the dark files

Line system

The convention of a survey sheet: contours are fine, and an index contour is drawn heavier so the eye can count them. Here the index is the pass’s own contour, so the crossing is the heaviest place in the mark.

Contour
max(1, 1.2 (N/256)0.6) device px: 1 at 180, 1.82 at 512, 2.76 at 1024. Never under 1 px.
Index contour
2 × a contour: the pass’s own figure-eight.
The point
3.25 × the index across, on the crossing.

Construction

ridge
22.5°, a sixteenth of a turn
summit
circle A, RA = 16, |AC| = 2 RA
crossing
two axes through C at ±ψ to the ridge, sin ψ = 4/5 (53.1°)
branches
each the arc tangent to its axis at C and tangent inside to its lobe’s circle: Q = 5/2 R
knoll
the summit’s lobe scaled by −1/2 through C: RB = RA / 2, |BC| = 2 RB
interval
d = RA / 4; the summit ends 4 d up, the knoll 2 d

Every curve is a circle or an arc that joins one on a tangent. The whole sheet is in logo/CONSTRUCTION.md.

Clear space and smallest sizes

Keep half the mark’s height clear on every side, of the mark alone and of a lockup. Nothing else, words, edges or other marks, comes inside it. The smallest mark is 16 px, the tab icon. The smallest lockup has a 20 px mark.

Lockups

The name in Spline Sans Mono at 540, outlined in the files so it never depends on the face being installed. Horizontal: the caps are half the mark’s height, centred on the tile, and the L’s stem stands 0.4 M from it. Stacked: the word at 0.4 M, its caps 0.25 M under the tile. The -small files are drawn for a 26 px mark, as in the site’s header.

03Type

One face, Spline Sans Mono.

04Interface

The website’s own components.

Pick a reader and the map shows what they can see. Press an area to seal it from them.

05The map

Land, sea and borders.

The map is the brand’s one illustration. A company is an island of plain pale land on a grey sea, its areas divided by dashed borders like counties. A sealed area is hatched and stamped with a wax seal. When a copy goes live the sea turns blue. No pictures, gradients or relief: hairlines and flat fills.

  • Land --land: an area you can read
  • Sea --sea, with a faint --grid
  • Border --dash, a dashed hairline
  • Sealed --sealed-land, hatched, a wax seal
  • Live --live-sea, and the --live dot
  • Names in the one face; where a name goes depends only on the map and the zoom

06Words

Plain words, from the reader’s side of the screen.

Locus is Latin for “place”: the spot on a map where something is. It’s the one place an agent goes to learn where everything in a company is, and the rules that apply.

The name

product
Locus, with a capital L, in running text and in the lockup.
line
A map of your company that any agent can read.
longer
A map of your company that any agent can read. Encrypted, gated, open source.
package
locus-harness on npm. The command it installs is locus.
never
npx locus: that name on npm belongs to someone else, and would run their code with a reader’s password. Write npx -y locus-harness.

How it talks

  • Short, direct sentences, in the active voice. Say what happens.
  • A button says exactly what it does: Go live, Copy, Seal.
  • Name things as people know them, never as the code does.
  • Facts, not reassurance: “The host stores only ciphertext.”
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no “simply”.

The screen’s words and the code’s

Change what the screen says freely; the code keeps its own names.

Say

  • Each reader gets a password that opens only the areas you choose.
  • Secrets are removed before anything leaves.
  • Change 1 secret found in a file

Not

  • Leverage our AI-powered knowledge platform!
  • Gate 3 has a piece with an unresolved service ref.
  • Oops! Something went wrong.

07Files

Everything, ready to take.

This folder, brand/, is the pack. Take files from it rather than redrawing anything. It is written by one command, npm run brand, from brand/source/ and the app’s stylesheets, and a test fails if the pack and the app ever disagree.

For agents

brand.json lists every file with its use, every colour with its meaning, the type and the rules. README.md says the same for people. Tokens are in tokens/tokens.json (Design Tokens format) and tokens/tokens.css; the mark’s geometry and its score are in logo/tiers.json.

Choosing a mark

Pick by the device pixels it will be drawn at (CSS px × the screen’s density), then the ground: light on a light page, dark on a dark one. A tab icon is always favicon.ico. A README header is the stacked lockup.
