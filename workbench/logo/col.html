Col

A summit, a lower knoll and the col between them, drawn in contour. The col’s own contour crosses itself at the pass, and the point sits on the crossing: Locus is the one place two heights share, where your company and an agent meet. Every curve is a circle or the arc that joins two on a tangent. Below 96 device pixels the mark is its own drawing: one solid land with the col’s own contour cut through it. It moves once, growing from nothing, and then holds still.

Every drawing on this page is chosen by device pixels, as a screen would choose it: this screen draws at ×. The only motion is the growth: it plays once as the mark appears, and again when Motion is switched on; then every line holds still. With reduced motion asked for, it never plays. Every mark here is drawn by col.js, the same file the shots and the exports use.
